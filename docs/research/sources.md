# Research sources

Articles are original diagnostic writing. They cite public failure documentation. They do not copy Haynes or Toyota workshop manuals.

## Hilux

- [Carify: Toyota Hilux common problems](https://www.carify.com.au/toyota/hilux/problems) for 1KD injector seats, EGR carbon, and the 2015-2020 1GD DPF Federal Court window
- [Craig Jones Auto: 1KD-FTV reliability](https://craigjonesauto.com.au/toyota-hilux-common-problems-report/) for turbo, intercooler hoses, and oil dilution
- [Unsealed 4x4: N80 problems](https://unsealed4x4.com.au/toyota-hilux-n80-common-problems/) for fifth injector, EGR cooler, airbox dust, timing-chain rattle, trans cooler, door drains, thin paint
- [Toyota Europe Hilux Hybrid 48V](https://newsroom.toyota.eu/new-hilux-hybrid-48v-enhances-an-invincible-icon/) for belt motor-generator, 48V pack, DC-DC
- [Drive: 2024 HiLux V-Active](https://www.drive.com.au/news/2024-toyota-hilux-mild-hybrid-tech-details/) for Australian naming and grade fitment
- [4x4 Australia: 2026 HiLux](https://www.4x4australia.com.au/news/2026-toyota-hilux-revealed) for V-Active continuing on higher grades
- [Toyota Australia HiLux spec table](https://media.adtorqueedge.com/new-cars/toyota-au/hilux/hilux-specs.pdf) for 1GD-FTV 48V and manual DPF burn switch

## Method

- Prefer a test that can fail a cheap part before a dear one (12V before 48V pack, hose before turbo, filter before high-pressure pump).
- If a factory torque or rail-pressure number is not public, the article says so and gives the measurement method.
- DPF delete is not listed as a repair.
